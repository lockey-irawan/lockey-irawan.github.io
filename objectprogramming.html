Object Oriented Programming

The Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) course introduces both the basic and advanced principles of object-oriented software development. Throughout the semester, students will engage in a comprehensive study of Class Structure, Encapsulation, Inheritance, and Polymorphism. The curriculum further advances into complex programming components, including Interfaces, Abstract Classes, Generics, Collections, and Exceptions. Ultimately, this course aims to equip students with both the theoretical knowledge and practical skills necessary to systematically design and implement highly modular, efficient, and easily maintainable software architectures.
